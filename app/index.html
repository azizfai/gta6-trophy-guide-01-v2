    Roadmap to the Platinum

    Four stages, in order. Finish the story first, then the duo work and stranger strands, then the open-world grind, then cleanup. The platinum pops when the last trophy does.

    1. Stage 1 — Story (11 trophies)

      Play Chapters 1–4 and the final mission. Earns the chapter trophies, the mission trophies (Gone Fishing, Breakfast Run, Child’s Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory), the Cartel Heist, the final-mission gold, and the platinum's foundation. Before finishing Chapter 1, meditate at the Chapter 1 meditation spot — it may not come back (see Positive Mental Attitude).

    2. Stage 2 — Heists & duo work (14 trophies)

      Stranger strands for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, plus Bret's hunt, until 8 strands are complete (Strangers & Freaks silver). Then the duo robberies with your partner: bank vaults, armored truck, jewelry store, shops back-to-back, losing a 4-star wanted level together, and one illegal operation per gang.

    3. Stage 3 — Open-world grind (27 trophies)

      The long tail: 250 stunts, 42 gold Shooting Range medals, 14 drone-race wins, 20 race wins, 100 vehicles, 52 areas, 100 WAiNK It locations, 3 card sets, 18 meditation spots, 50 challenges, fishing vs Cal, arcade, bowling, the Ocean Beach cocktail, the showroom smash, the VCIA departure, the melee drive-by, $250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent, 10 Scooter Bros registrations, and the 5 still-unconfirmed secret trophies once their methods are confirmed.

    4. Stage 4 — Cleanup & 100% (3 trophies)

      Buy all 8 garage properties, reach 100% completion (A Long Road, gold), and the platinum — Vice Got a Price! — pops with the final trophy. Use the tracker's filters to find the last stragglers.

    Missable & Unconfirmed

    Possibly missable — 1 trophy

    Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot across Leonida): one meditation spot sits in the Chapter 1 area and may vanish after Chapter 1 ends. Meditate there before you finish Chapter 1. Every other trophy in this guide is earned in free roam after the story and cannot be missed.

    Still unconfirmed — 5 trophies

    Their methods are not confirmed by any source we checked, so this guide marks them UNCONFIRMED instead of inventing a method: Challenging Tank, Personal Best, Actions Speak Louder, Bad Luck, I Guess, and The Truth Is Out There. Check PowerPyx’s GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap or the GTA Zone trophy list for updates before chasing them.
